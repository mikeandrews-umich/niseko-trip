The mountain

Siberian air crosses the Sea of Japan and drops some of the lightest, driest snow anywhere on Hokkaido, about 50 feet a season. Niseko United is four resorts and 2,191 acres on the Ikon Pass, and January is the deepest month.

  • The four resortsGrand Hirafu (right above the houses), Hanazono, Niseko Village and Annupuri, linked over the top.
  • Backcountry gatesNiseko's gates open terrain past the boundary. Only go through with avalanche gear, a partner and the know-how, or book a guided tour. When a gate is closed, it's closed.
  • Worth findingStrawberry Fields at Hanazono for trees, the Pizza Box lift, and Boyoso.
  • Night skiingHirafu keeps runs lit after dark, and it's included with your pass.

FAQ

Not here? Ask in the group chat.

Which airport do I fly into?

New Chitose (CTS), outside Sapporo. Land by 3 PM on Sunday Jan 3 to make the 4 PM charter bus.

I'm landing late or on another day. Now what?

You'll get to Niseko on your own. Airport buses and private transfers run from CTS. Tell the logistics team your plan so we know when you're arriving.

How do I sign up for add-ons and dinners?

Both are tabs in one Google Sheet, linked under Sign-ups. Type your name in an open green cell.

Do I need a visa?

U.S. passport holders don't need one for a short tourist trip. Other passports vary, so check Japan's visa-exemption list early. International students: make sure your U.S. visa and I-20 are in order for getting back in.

Do I need an Ikon Pass?

Niseko United is on the Ikon Pass. Pass and lift-ticket details: TBA.

I've never skied. Is that OK?

Yes. Ski or board, either works. Book group lessons directly with GoSnow, and Niseko's beginner runs are wide and soft.

Cash or card?

Cards work most places in Hirafu, but bring yen for small restaurants, buses and onsen. ATMs at Lawson and 7-Eleven take foreign cards.

Will my charger work?

Yes. Japan uses the same plugs as the U.S. Bring a battery pack, because phones die fast in the cold.
